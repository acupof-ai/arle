# Qwen3.6-27B FP8 + MTP on H20 — CUDA, 2026-07-15

> Status: Shipped

## Goal

Validate Qwen3.6-27B (FP8 dense, hybrid linear+full attention) inference on a single H20 GPU with MTP speculative decode, measuring decode throughput at c=1,4,8 concurrency and MTP draft token counts of 2 and 4.

## Hypothesis

MTP (the model's built-in NextN draft head) with greedy verification should provide a modest decode speedup over baseline greedy decode, with higher draft token counts yielding more speedup.

## Parameters

```
Model: Qwen/Qwen3.6-27B-FP8 (FP8 quantized)
Backend: CUDA, single GPU (no TP)
MTP: --spec-type mtp --mtp-draft-tokens {2,4} --mtp-draft-topk 1
Max tokens: 200 per request
Trials: 3 per data point
```

- Baseline: no MTP (greedy decode only)
- Treatment 1: MTP with 2 draft tokens, greedy verification (topk=1)
- Treatment 2: MTP with 4 draft tokens, greedy verification (topk=1)

## Environment

- Host: 8×H20 box
- GPU: 1× NVIDIA H20 (96 GB), CUDA 12.9
- Model: Qwen3.6-27B-FP8
- TP/EP: 1/1 (single GPU)
- Server flags: `--port 8800 --bind 0.0.0.0`

## Results

| concurrency | no MTP | MTP×2 | MTP×2 delta | MTP×4 | MTP×4 delta |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 39.8 tok/s | 41.4 tok/s | +4.0% | 41.4 tok/s | +4.0% |
| 4 | 63.4 tok/s | 64.9 tok/s | +2.4% | 65.0 tok/s | +2.5% |
| 8 | 86.8 tok/s | 88.1 tok/s | +1.5% | 87.8 tok/s | +1.2% |

TTFT: ~0.44s (MTP) vs ~0.49s (no MTP) at c=1.

Raw artifacts: curl-based timing, 3 trials per data point.

## Problems

- MTP improvement is modest at greedy topk=1 (1.5–4%); the draft head's predictions are frequently rejected when they diverge from greedy.
- MTP×4 does not significantly outperform MTP×2 at greedy topk=1, suggesting the 3rd and 4th draft tokens have low acceptance rates.

## Learnings

PASS. The 27B FP8 model runs correctly with MTP speculative decode on H20. MTP provides a consistent but small speedup at greedy verification. Next wall: test with topk>1 to increase draft acceptance rate, and compare against the 35B-A3B MoE model which has a different linear/full-attention ratio.
